🎙️ Candidate Interview Pitch: Project CIP-Shield

1. How to State the Problem (30-Second Hook)

"Sir/Ma'am, across over 75% of global factories running Rockwell ControlLogix PLCs, plant managers face the Deterministic Security Dilemma. When you enable standard CIP Security with TLS encryption on legacy controllers, the CPU overhead spikes latency to 30ms. That breaches the 1ms cyclic Requested Packet Interval (RPI), tripping the watchdog and shutting down the production line. To keep the factory running, operators disable encryption—leaving the plant vulnerable to CVE-2024-6242 slot bypass and CVE-2026-9637 DoS attacks."

2. How to Explain Your CIP-Shield Solution

"My proposed solution is CIP-Shield—an inline edge micro-proxy using Linux eBPF (Extended Berkeley Packet Filter) and hardware-accelerated ChaCha20-Poly1305 micro-tagging. It offloads all cryptographic inspection from the PLC. When an exploit packet is intercepted, our Synthetic Keep-Alive Synthesizer quarantees the threat and immediately feeds a valid synthetic status beacon to the PLC backplane. The assembly line keeps running at 100% throughput with zero jitter, while an encrypted alert is pushed to FactoryTalk DataMosaix."

"This avoids $2.6 Million per year in unplanned downtime per automotive plant and extends the life of Rockwell's massive brownfield installed base without requiring a multi-million-dollar chassis rip-and-replace."
