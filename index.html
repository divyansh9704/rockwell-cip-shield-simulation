1. How to State the Problem (30-Second Hook)

"Sir/Ma'am, across over 75% of global factories running Rockwell ControlLogix PLCs, plant managers face the Deterministic Security Dilemma. That breaches the 1.0ms cyclic Requested Packet Interval (RPI), tripping the 5.0ms hardware watchdog and shutting down the production line. To keep the factory running, operators disable encryption—leaving the plant vulnerable to CVE-2024-6242 slot bypass and CVE-2026-9637 DoS attacks."

"My proposed solution is CIP-Shield—an inline edge micro-proxy using Linux eBPF (Extended Berkeley Packet Filter) and hardware-accelerated ChaCha20-Poly1305 micro-tagging. It offloads all cryptographic inspection from the PLC. When an exploit packet is intercepted, our Synthetic Keep-Alive Synthesizer isolates the threat and immediately feeds a valid synthetic status beacon to the PLC backplane. The assembly line keeps running at 100% throughput with zero jitter, while an encrypted alert is pushed to FactoryTalk DataMosaix."
